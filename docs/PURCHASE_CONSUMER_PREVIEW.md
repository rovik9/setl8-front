## Contract and implementation boundary

The reviewed additive backend declaration is copied to `src/api/purchase-foundation-v1.d.ts`, source SHA-256 **e2a5cd5b8a69699646036e2f4492ebc27b6566ddbb44de96fbbecdcf4a8a529b**. This is a distinct snapshot from the historical `purchase-draft-v1.d.ts` design copy (**f11be193cba2a2cc799f9952d04487ab310fb930c281222bdfdff64f8e6dac9f**), which remains untouched. No claim of equality to future backend revisions is made.

- `src/purchase/consumer/runtime.ts` bounds JSON-like input to 256 KiB, 10,000 nodes, depth 32, 8,192-character strings and 1,024 array entries before schema validation. DTO-specific limits include 20 offers, 100 rows/page, 64 evidence entries and 32 reasons. Exact-key checks reject unexpected fields, accessors, cycles and non-JSON/prototype values. Canonical decimal u64 strings, canonical base58 keys/signatures and 32-byte hex identities remain strings; BigInt checks the exact ceil fee and total. Runtime checks bind wallet, deployment/genesis/program/manifest, evidence purpose, approved mint/ruleset, public payment identities and account encodings. Snapshot contexts are two observations with a minimum-slot floor; they are not described as one exact bank snapshot.
- `client.ts` accepts a transport explicitly. Its default transport rejects locally without fetch, route, auth, cookie, RPC or wallet assumptions. Catalogue, quote, attempt acknowledgment, status, recovery pages, owned pages and account details are separate operations. Scope captures wallet/deployment/session epoch/purpose; per-operation cancellation, generation checks and a five-second bound discard old reads. Real pending work drives the existing progress indicator.
- Catalogue and quote selection use parsed service values. Review displays exact six-decimal base, fee and total for the known test mint; SOL costs are explicitly unestimated. Quote counters/accounts are unreserved hints. A local deadline check does not attest fresh service or chain admission.
- Recording an example rechecks complete recovery, captures the quote, lazy-loads the already accepted unsigned preparation module, then rechecks scope, quote identity and deadline after asynchronous work. The public request/message hash is retained with the recovery lock before the transport acknowledgment. A mismatched acknowledgment or uncertain outcome keeps that identity and lock; it never creates an automatic retry or replacement payment.
- Status updates cannot change the original request/quote/account/message identity. Client report, chain observation, verification, indexing, current chain state and evaluator registration remain separate. A reported quote mismatch does not erase independently reported ownership. Terminal admission remains disabled and P30 records do not enter the old account terminal.
- Recovery and ownership lists each append only opaque pages at their own unchanged cutoff, refusing duplicates/no-progress or changed cutoffs. The controlled UI exposes continuation for both. Reads are bounded to 1,000 rows; ownership also has a ten-page cap. Partial/blocked discovery or missing history is explicitly incomplete, including an empty page. Missing uncertain attempts stay uncertain. This checkpoint deliberately offers no repayment/unlock path.

These are response-shape and context checks, not cryptographic proof of chain truth, token-account authority, current balances or purchase viability. Canonical instruction construction remains SDK-owned. The approved dev.5 module/pin and old dev.4 order codec are unchanged and independently guarded.

The fixture transport is injected only by the standalone entry; the consumer itself never falls back to fixtures. Fixture wallet changes clear prior material; scenario changes invalidate the review and refresh recovery/discovery. Existing branding, typography, navigation links and reduced-motion behavior are reused.

Scenarios cover ordinary progress, unknown submission, lost acknowledgment, quote expiry, quote mismatch and partial discovery. Two public identities are copied from accepted synthetic SDK vectors. The fixture stores records in memory and can model controller restart recovery while that service object survives; page/process reload is not durable recovery. No real wallet was used.
